Scope. Senate bills (S-numbers) from the 2025–26 session whose subject touches housing supply, zoning, land use, permitting and environmental review, building codes, housing finance, vouchers and rental assistance, tenant protections and rent regulation, shelter siting, and social or supportive housing. Assembly-only bills are not included; where a Senate bill has an Assembly companion it's listed as "Same as". Bills already signed, vetoed, stricken or defeated in committee are left out (for example S10442, the HDC bond cap increase, was signed July 31, 2026, and S5554, barring shelters near transit, was defeated in Cities 1).
Status. The Legislature adjourned in June; bills remain alive until the session ends December 31, 2026. Bills that "passed both houses" have not yet been delivered to the Governor.
Cosponsors. Taken from the current version of each bill on nysenate.gov. Where the Senate site hides part of a long list, rows are marked "list may be incomplete"; check the bill page before relying on an absence. Brad Hoylman-Sigal's bills remain on the record after his departure from the Senate.
Supporters. Groups from the team's Coalition Partners Database that have publicly backed a bill through a memo of support, a sign-on letter or a coalition platform. A missing name means no public record was found, not opposition.
Sources: NY Senate Open Legislation, bill pages linked from each number; bill discovery via Smith & Moses bill search.
