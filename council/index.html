Scope. Local law introductions (Int. numbers) from the 2026–29 Council session, using the same subjects as the Albany tracker: zoning and land use, permitting and environmental review, building code and construction, housing finance and production, vouchers and rental assistance, tenant protections and rent regulation, shelters and homelessness, and social, public and supportive housing. Resolutions are not included. Bills on building energy and emissions, worksite safety, sidewalk sheds, parking garages and other building topics that don't bear on housing supply, cost or tenancy are left out. Bills already enacted, vetoed, withdrawn or filed are left out (for example Int. 966, establishing a rental assistance voucher program, was enacted July 30, 2026; Int. 839, making the certification of no harassment program permanent, was enacted Sept. 12; and Int. 421, allowing cellar units in new one- and two-family homes, was enacted April 9).
Status. Council bills stay alive until the session ends December 31, 2029. "Heard and laid over" means the committee held a hearing but hasn't voted. Upcoming dates are committee meetings where the bill is on the agenda.
Sponsors. The first name listed on the Council's record is shown as prime sponsor; the rest are cosponsors. Public Advocate Jumaane Williams sponsors some bills and isn't part of the 51-member roster. Rows marked "list may be incomplete" include sponsors the data doesn't name, such as borough presidents; check the bill page.
